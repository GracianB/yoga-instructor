# Living Sanctuary

## Idea

Yoga Instructor is designed as a digital room rather than a brochure. Motion, sound, navigation and state changes are subordinate to reading and attention.

## The 10 phases

1. **Sanctuary direction**. Move the visual language from wellness cliché to editorial calm.
2. **Living hero**. Let ambient light and motion respond to presence without becoming a dashboard.
3. **Ritual states**. Arrive, Move, Focus and Share define four ways into the site.
4. **One Breath**. A 19-second 4–7–8 cycle lives inside the page as an optional practice.
5. **Presence field**. Pointer and scroll become low-amplitude environmental signals.
6. **Sound organ**. Playback changes a soft visual pulse instead of producing an equalizer.
7. **Memory**. The last practice is remembered locally, without forced navigation.
8. **Quiet Mode**. The visitor can intentionally remove most visual noise.
9. **Quality contracts**. JS syntax, HTML invariants, bilingual keys, assets and size budgets are checked in CI.
10. **Closure**. The repository is released with protected main and required quality checks.

## Principles

**No autoplay.** Sound is always a choice.

**No performance theatre.** Effects exist to support rhythm, not to demonstrate that the browser can move pixels.

**No profiling.** Local state stores only interface preferences and the last chosen practice.

**Reduced motion is a first-class state.** The experience remains usable when animation is unavailable.

**The user may leave.** Every interaction can be ignored, skipped or silenced.

## Sound direction

The selected acoustic reference is **Silence Between Notes**. The public player points to the selected Suno composition, while the interface treats the audio as atmosphere rather than a focal object.

The repository player remains deliberately non-autoplaying. Replacing the local MP3 with the exported master does not require changes to the experience engine.

## Portfolio role

Yoga is the human track.

Systems Lab demonstrates systems thinking and engineering discipline. Professional Deck communicates career narrative. Yoga demonstrates attention, teaching, embodiment and human experience.

The three should feel related, but never identical.
