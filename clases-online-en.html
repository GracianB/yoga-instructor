05 / Before we begin

You might be
wondering…

Can I begin without being flexible?

Yes. There is no particular pose you must reach. We'll explore variations and support, and you are free to rest.

When do sessions take place?

No fixed dates are advertised. Email me about availability. The calendar is only for an introductory conversation.

What do I need for an online class?

A stable connection, room to move and a mat. For the workshop, also have a blanket, cushion and an optional sturdy chair. The platform and link are confirmed after agreement.

Do I need to turn my camera on?

Video can help me offer movement guidance. We can discuss your preferences beforehand; without video, visual feedback is limited.

How is a place confirmed?

We agree on availability, price and terms by email. An email or calendar conversation is only the first contact, not a reservation.
